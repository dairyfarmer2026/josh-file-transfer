JOSH FILE TRANSFER

Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. This policy covers the owner's personal-use rclone connection and this information site.

Information accessed

The connection accesses Google Drive folder and file metadata, such as names, identifiers, sizes, and modification times, and file contents needed for transfers requested by the owner. Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on the owner's server to permit authorized access. The connection does not request or store the owner's Google password.

How information is used

Drive information is used to locate project folders, transfer selected files between Drive and the owner's workspace or another destination selected by the owner, and verify transfer results. Operational records may include filenames, transfer results, and errors.

Storage and sharing

Transferred files may remain in the owner's workspace, Google Drive, or other owner-selected storage until the owner removes them. OAuth credentials remain in the owner's access-controlled server configuration. Files and metadata are not published by this information site. Data is not sold or used for advertising. Data may be processed by Google, the owner's hosting or storage providers, and other destinations or tools explicitly selected by the owner to carry out a requested operation.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This file-transfer connection does not use Google user data to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Revocation and deletion

The owner may revoke Google access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically remove copies already transferred. The owner can remove those copies, operational records, and saved OAuth credentials from the systems where they are stored.

This public site

This static site has no sign-in form, analytics scripts, or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process normal web-request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Technical questions can be raised through the project support page. Do not include private files or credentials. Changes to this connection's data practices will be reflected on this page.
